About

Hello, I'm Brooke.

I'm Brooke Hayley, a designer, artist, singer and facilitator based in Ōtautahi / Christchurch. Stone to Sky brings together my creative work, my relationship with nature and the people I work alongside.

My work moves between worlds: digital and natural, creative and ceremonial, personal and collective. I am most at home when a project asks for care, depth, and a visual language that reflects the more-than-human world.

Work together View work
“There is a creative flame that has burned and flourished throughout my life. It's taken many forms and continues to evolve as I do. My work is a devoted prayer to the Earth, to beauty, and to the cycles that hold us.”

What guides me

Nature, creativity and connection

Beyond the creative work, I am a ceremonial facilitator and student of sacred medicine paths. My connection with nature runs through everything I create - I tend gardens as living temples and learn from plants as teachers.

My musical offerings - voice, guitar, drum, and ethereal soundscapes - share stories from my journey of opening and remembering. I have sung with Ora.Vida.Life, contributed to sound-healing projects across Aotearoa, and supported retreats as a ceremonial musician.

My practice

What I offer

Brand Design - identities and design systems for soul-led businesses, small studios, and community ventures.

Illustration - artwork that honours the seasons, the land, and the subtle intelligence of nature.

Voice & Facilitation - ceremonial song and facilitation for circles, retreats, and sacred spaces.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Brand Design, Illustration, and simple websites for soul-led businesses, plus Voice & Facilitation for retreats, gatherings, and community spaces.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Most creative projects happen online with clear check-ins. Working remotely across New Zealand, Australia, and internationally.

Can Brand Design, Illustration, and web design be bundled?

Yes. Many clients mix offerings, from brand identity to illustration and a simple website, to create a cohesive visual world.

How does the collaboration process work?

We start with a call to confirm alignment, then move through discovery, concepts, and refinement with clear milestones.

How do you price your creative work?

Projects are quoted based on depth and scope. Brand Design and web packages are tiered; Illustration and Voice & Facilitation are priced individually. Payment plans are available.

Do you offer one-on-one voice or creativity sessions?

Yes. I hold gentle sessions focused on nervous-system safety, vocal expression, and reconnecting with your natural voice.

Can you support my retreat or workshop?

I offer Voice & Facilitation, ceremony support, and visual design for event materials. Each space is shaped around the group's intention and capacity.

What style of brands do you work best with?

Earth-based, soulful, nature-aligned projects. I work best with collaborative, values-led clients who want depth over trend.

Can you create custom illustrations for my project?

Yes. I design one-off illustrations, botanical drawings, and symbolic pieces for brands, websites, packaging, and personal commissions.

I am still clarifying my idea - is that okay?

Absolutely. Many clients come with a seed of an idea. I help shape language, visuals, and structure so the offering feels grounded and true.

Are you a fit for fast turnarounds or trend-led launches?

I am likely not the best match for rush timelines, trend-first direction, or price-first decisions. I work best with spacious timelines and shared trust.
