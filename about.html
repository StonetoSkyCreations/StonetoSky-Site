About Brooke

I am a designer, artist, singer, and facilitator based in Ōtautahi / Christchurch. Stone to Sky is where those strands meet, held by a steady devotion to beauty, clarity, and the rhythms of the Earth.

Introduction

A home for three strands

My work moves between worlds - digital and natural, creative and ceremonial, personal and collective. I am most at home when a project asks for care, depth, and a visual language that reflects the more-than-human world.

Brand Design is the central thread, with Illustration and Voice & Facilitation woven in when it brings a project to life.

What I weave

The three strands

Brand Design

Visual identities and design systems for soul-led businesses, small studios, and community ventures.

Illustration

Artwork that honors the seasons, the land, and the subtle intelligence of nature.

Voice & Facilitation

Ceremonial song and facilitation for circles, retreats, and sacred spaces.

Deeper roots

What guides me

Beyond my creative work, I am a ceremonial facilitator and student of sacred medicine paths. My connection with nature runs through everything I create. I tend gardens as living temples and learn from plants as teachers.

My musical offerings - voice, guitar, drum, and ethereal soundscapes - share stories from my journey of opening and remembering. I have sung with Ora.Vida.Life, contributed to sound-healing projects across Aotearoa, and supported retreats as a ceremonial musician.

My work is grounded in service to the land, to beauty, and to the creative spark that moves through all things.

"There is a creative flame that has burned and flourished throughout my life. It's taken many forms and continues to evolve as I do. My work is a devoted prayer to the Earth, to beauty, and to the cycles that hold us." Stone to Sky
