About Stone to Sky

Stone to Sky is led by Brooke, a Brand Designer and Illustrator, with Voice & Facilitation offered by invitation. Brooke leads creative direction and works collaboratively with clients. Working remotely across New Zealand, Australia, and internationally.

The work sits between land, story, and the quiet intelligence of craft.

Every project is delivered with structure, clarity, and a documented handover.
