Voice & Facilitation - Gatherings

Voice, ceremony, and community circles

My voice work lives in circles, ceremonies, and small gatherings. It is invitational, grounded, and shaped around the needs of the group.

  • Workshops for voice, breath, and presence
  • Ceremonial song for retreats and thresholds
  • Community circles and seasonal gatherings
  • One-on-one sessions for nervous-system safety and vocal expression

A gathering moves through grounded arrival, song and exploration, and integration - closing with reflection and practices to continue at home. Best for grounded, consent-led spaces with time to listen and co-create.
