Voice / Facilitation

Invitation-based sessions are shaped around the gathering size, preparation, and care required.

Availability is seasonal and intentionally limited.

Sliding scale or gifted work is reserved for exceptional, deeply aligned non-profit projects.

Fit

Gentle friction for better alignment.

This section clarifies fit for values-led founders; the next step is to continue if aligned and pause if not.

Good fit if you:

  • Value slow, considered craft over quick production.
  • Want a Brand Designer who leads creative direction.
  • Prefer collaboration that includes honest guidance.
  • Are building something with long-term intent.

Not a fit if you:

  • Need fast turnarounds or high-volume production.
  • Want to control every creative decision.
  • Are choosing solely on price or trend alignment.
  • Need instant quotes or pre-set timelines.
