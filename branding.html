FAQs

Real questions, real answers.

FAQs exist to clarify scope, timelines, and fit.

How long does a Brand Design project usually take?

Most projects take 6-12 weeks, depending on scope and feedback pace. Timelines are agreed collaboratively once scope is clear.

What if I need just a logo?

Brand Design works best as a system. If you only need a logo, we can discuss a smaller Foundation package to ensure the mark still has context and support.

How many revisions are included?

Revisions are built into each stage so we can refine with clarity. The goal is collaboration, not unlimited change rounds.

Can I bring my own ideas?

Yes. You bring your vision and insight, and Brooke leads creative direction to translate that into a cohesive system.

What is the investment range?

Most Brand Design projects sit in the mid-four figures to low five figures NZD. Packages are set prices with flexibility when scope or reciprocal exchange calls for it.

Do you take rush projects?

No. This work is intentionally paced to protect quality and creative integrity.
