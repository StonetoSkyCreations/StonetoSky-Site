FAQs

Illustration questions answered.

These answers clarify licensing, timelines, and how to begin.

How long does an illustration commission take?

Timelines range from 2-6 weeks depending on complexity and usage. Timelines are agreed collaboratively once scope is clear.

Do you offer exclusive licensing?

Yes. Exclusive licensing and buyouts are available when required, and priced according to usage.

Can you match an existing brand style?

Yes. We can align illustration with your existing Brand Design system while keeping the artwork distinctive.

Do you take rush work?

No rush production. Quality illustration needs time for iteration and refinement.
